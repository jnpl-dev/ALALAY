## Scoring Guide

For reference only. This section is not printed on the questionnaire handed to respondents.

**Overall SUS Score:**

For each item, compute the contribution:

contribution = (response − 1)

Sum all 10 contributions (range 0–40). Multiply by 2.5.

Overall score = Sum × 2.5 (range 0–100)

**Per-Criterion Score:**

Average the raw (1–5) scores of the items within each criterion group.

| Criterion | Items | Range |
|---|---|---|
| Ease of Use | 1, 2, 3 | 1–5 |
| Efficiency | 4, 5 | 1–5 |
| Learnability | 6, 7, 8 | 1–5 |
| User Satisfaction | 9, 10 | 1–5 |
